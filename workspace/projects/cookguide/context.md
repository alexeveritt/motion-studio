# CookGuide context

> Written for Motion Studio's acceptance production. Items marked **To confirm** need the owner's input before anything is published.

## Product

CookGuide guides people through a recipe step by step while they cook.

**To confirm:** full product description, platforms, availability and pricing.

## Audience

Home cooks who want help following a recipe, including people deciding what to cook tonight.

## Terminology

- CookGuide is one word with a capital C and G.
- Say "recipe steps", not "instructions".

## Claims that may be made

- CookGuide gives step-by-step recipe guidance.

## Claims that must not be made

- Health, nutrition, weight-loss or medical claims.
- Specific time or money savings.
- "Free", app-store availability or pricing, until confirmed.
- Endorsements, awards, ratings or user numbers.

## Tone

Warm, practical and encouraging. Short sentences. No hype or exclamation marks.
